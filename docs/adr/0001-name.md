# ADR 0001: Name — Janus

## Status

Accepted.

## Context

The project needs a name for a market agent that does two things at once:

- remembers what you believed, what you knew, and how it turned out;
- turns that memory into calibrated probabilities about what comes next.

The name must be concrete, easy to say, and free of the usual abstractions
("Alpha", "Signal", "Insight") and the usual cuteness ("OpenFinch").

## Decision

Use **Janus** (雅努斯).

Janus is the Roman god of gates, doorways, transitions, beginnings, and endings.
He is depicted with two faces because every threshold is both an ending and a
beginning. January is named after him.

The two faces are not duplicity. They are the two disciplines of the product:
**Memory** and **Foresight**.

## Taglines

- Primary: **Look back to reason forward.**
- Secondary: **Past in memory. Future in probabilities.**
- Chinese: **回望以推理，记忆以预测。**

## Why it works

- Real word, concrete image, two syllables.
- The myth maps to the product without a metaphor stretch: gates, transitions,
  markets, regime changes.
- It holds both halves of the loop in one image, so the name does not collapse
  into "review tool" or "prediction tool".
- It is an instrument, not an oracle. Janus does not promise the future; it
  stands at the threshold and reasons about it.

## What to avoid

- **"Two-faced"** in English means deceitful. Never use it in product copy.
  Use "two faces", "looks both ways", or "memory and foresight".
- **"Remembers backward"** is not idiomatic. Memory is already backward.
- Do not sell prediction. Sell probabilities, evidence, and calibration.

## Consequences

- The two faces map directly to the Memory and Foresight layers.
- The "gate" metaphor fits market transitions and regime changes.
- Collisions (Janus Henderson, Janus Gateway, JanusGraph) are in adjacent
  spaces. Disambiguate with the subtitle "a market agent with memory and
  foresight" and the topics `market-agent`, `memory`, `forecasting`,
  `calibration`, `point-in-time`.
- If a trademark issue appears, the product can be presented as "Janus Market"
  while the wordmark remains "Janus".
