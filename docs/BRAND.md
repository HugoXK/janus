# Brand: Janus

## Name

**Janus** (雅努斯).

## One-liner

A market agent with memory and foresight.

## Elevator pitch

Janus is a market agent with two faces: **Memory** records what you believed,
what you knew, and how it turned out; **Foresight** turns that record into
calibrated probabilities about what comes next. It is local-first,
point-in-time, and built to be audited.

## The myth, accurately

- Janus is the Roman god of **gates, doorways, transitions, beginnings, and
  endings**.
- He is shown with two faces because a threshold looks both ways: what is
  ending, and what is beginning.
- **January** is named after him: the month where the old year closes and the
  new one opens.
- The two faces are **not** duplicity. They are memory and foresight.

## Voice

**Instrument, not oracle.**

- Calm, precise, evidence-first.
- Short sentences. One idea per sentence. No hype.
- Say "probability", not "prediction".
- Say "evidence", not "signal".
- Say "calibrate", not "guarantee".

**Use:** remember, record, reason, forecast, calibrate, review, gate, threshold,
memory, evidence, probability, scenario, regime, provenance.

**Avoid:** predict, guarantee, beat the market, alpha, signal, two-faced, AI
stock picks, "never wrong", "the next big thing".

## Messaging hierarchy

1. **What it is:** a market agent with memory and foresight.
2. **Why it matters:** most tools either drown you in data or sell you a
   prediction.
3. **What it does:** records beliefs, computes evidence, forecasts
   probabilities, calibrates outcomes.
4. **Why trust it:** point-in-time, provenance, local-first, actually open.
5. **What it is not:** not advice, not execution, not a stock picker.

## Logo and wordmark

- A single vertical **gate line** with two mirrored profiles: one face made of
  data ticks, one face made of a probability curve.
- Monochrome first. One accent: amber (market) or teal (memory).
- Avoid Roman busts, literal columns, and "two-faced" imagery.
- Wordmark: **JANUS** in a quiet serif or mono; let the name breathe.
- Small-size mark: a double "J" mirrored around a gate line.

## Naming principles for future sub-brands

- Real word, concrete image, one or two syllables.
- Maps to the product's action, not the technology.
- No `Open-` prefix. "Open" is a promise, not a name.
- No abstract finance words: Alpha, Signal, Insight, Edge.
- No cute animals unless the animal's behavior *is* the product. Fledge works
  because a young bird leaving the nest is the product. A random bird is not.
